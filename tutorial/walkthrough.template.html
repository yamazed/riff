Pointing an API client at riff

riff is one proxy serving every client at once. Anything told to use 127.0.0.1:8888 shows up in the same list, and a client configured directly cannot be overridden by Zscaler.

Good to know

Before you share this with the team

Wait for security approval

riff intercepts TLS the way every interception proxy does: it generates a certificate authority on your machine and asks you to trust it. That is a real change to your trust store \u2014 read riff ca uninstall before you start, so you know how to undo it.

Undo everything

riff.exe proxy off
riff.exe ca uninstall
rmdir /s "%USERPROFILE%\.riff"

Nothing is installed system-wide and no admin rights were used, so removal is just these three lines.

The private key never moves

Each person gets their own CA under %USERPROFILE%\.riff. Never put RIFF_HOME somewhere other people can read: a shared key would let any of them impersonate any HTTPS site to everyone who trusts it.

What riff will not do

  • HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 are not supported. riff offers only http/1.1, so clients negotiate down.
  • Hosts outside your decrypt list are tunnelled opaquely. Sign-in traffic stays unreadable by design.
  • Rules are a sandbox: no shell, no imports, no network, no file access beyond an explicit save.
